Gold Motion Extra offline AI models

GM Anime
- Architecture/weights: lightweight Real-ESRGAN Anime x4 (4B32F)
- Runtime file: gm_anime_x4.tflite
- Conversion source: https://huggingface.co/FuryTMP/RealESR_Animex4_fp16
- The fixed 128px TFLite conversion preserves the trained network. Its nearest-neighbor
  residual branch is expressed with mathematically equivalent built-in Concat and
  DepthToSpace operations so Android does not require TensorFlow Flex ops.

GM Soul
- Architecture/weights: realesr-general-x4v3
- Runtime file: gm_soul_x4.tflite
- Conversion source: https://huggingface.co/JoPmt/Real_Esrgan_x2_Onnx_Tflite_Tfjs
- Upstream project: https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN
- The same built-in-only residual resize substitution described above is used.

Both runtime files are distinct models and are executed fully offline through
the TensorFlow Lite runtime already bundled with Gold Motion.

BG Cut
- Architecture: U2-NetP / U2-Net Lite saliency segmentation
- Runtime file: u2netp_lite.tflite (320x320 float32, seven side outputs)
- Source artifact: https://huggingface.co/abhimanyu666/u2nettflite
